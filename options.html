Moodle site

Any Moodle 4.x site with the Moodle app enabled works. Changing the site clears tasks and the connection for the old one.

Connection

Connect signs in with your usual university login, the same way the Moodle app does. The token stays on this computer only. You can revoke it any time on the site under Preferences > Security keys.
